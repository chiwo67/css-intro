Introduction

Green energy is a crucial aspect of sustainable development. It encompasses various renewable energy sources that help reduce carbon emissions and combat climate change.
This report shows the annual growth and resource types available for green energy development.

Solar Energy

Solar energy uses sunlight to generate electricity through solar panels and solar thermal systems.

  • Solar panels convert sunlight into electricity using photovoltaic cells.
  • Solar power is clean, renewable, and widely available in sunny regions.
  • It is used for homes, businesses, solar farms, and off-grid applications.
  • Improvements in solar technology are increasing efficiency and lowering costs.

Wind Energy

Wind turbines convert wind into electrical energy using rotor blades and generators.

Geothermal Energy

Geothermal energy uses heat from beneath the Earth’s surface to produce power and heat buildings.
